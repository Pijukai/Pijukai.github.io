Improving Attention in Egocentric World Models

Work in progress

  • World models
  • Diffusion
  • Attention
  • Register tokens

Objective
Make diffusion world models for egocentric driving cheaper to train and run, by replacing full attention with more efficient mechanisms.
Status
Work in progress: targeting reduced training and inference time.

The project

With the EPFL VITA Lab, I investigate different attention mechanisms, such as register-token attention (à la VGGT-Omega), as an efficient replacement for full attention in diffusion world models for egocentric driving, targeting reduced training and inference time.

This project is still ongoing. More details and results will be added here as the work progresses.
