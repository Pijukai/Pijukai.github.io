About Me

Hi, I’m Julien

01

Student

I’d describe myself as an autonomous, rigorous and curious student. Curiosity is also why I ended my Bachelor’s with 196 credits instead of the required 180: there were simply too many interesting subjects to leave out, and I’m not afraid of hard work.

EPFL also gave me a group of friends with whom I’ve made countless memories. We learned a lot from each other and went through the toughest academic challenges together.

02

ML Researcher and Engineer

I’ve worked on many projects, across very different domains and with many collaborators, from decoding brain signals to simulating particle physics and dark matter. Whenever possible, I try to challenge myself technically.

I’m always eager to pick up new skills, tips and insights that deepen my understanding of the field.

03

Music Lover

I love a lot of music, from funk to lofi, depending on the moment. Music is a precious tool for me: to decompress, to focus, or simply to enjoy every moment. The lofi you can turn on with the speaker button is a small taste of it.

04

Gamer

I’m a big fan of gaming in all its forms: strategy games like Civilization, indie games and their endless creativity and ability to surprise everyone, and big franchises like Assassin’s Creed.

And I’m an absolute fan of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33.
